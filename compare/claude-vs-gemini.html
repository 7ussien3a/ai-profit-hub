🏆 OUR VERDICT

Winner: Tie — Claude for Writing, Gemini for Research

There's no clear winner here — it depends on what you need. Claude excels at long-form writing, deep analysis, and nuanced content. Gemini dominates in multimodal tasks, real-time research, and anything that benefits from Google Search integration. Pick the one that matches your workflow.

💬 Hussein's Take
