🏆 OUR VERDICT

Winner: Perplexity for Research, ChatGPT for Everything Else

These tools serve fundamentally different purposes. Perplexity is the best AI search engine — it gives you cited, factual answers fast. ChatGPT is the best all-around AI assistant — it writes, codes, generates images, and handles creative tasks. Most power users will want both.
