Editorial Role

Hussein Harby founded AI Profit Hub and is the named editor responsible for its current publication decisions. His work includes choosing topics, reviewing source attribution, distinguishing provider claims from editorial analysis, and deciding whether a page should remain indexed.

Editorial Principles

  • Evidence before confidence: Material facts should link to primary documentation or clearly identified independent reporting.
  • Honest review labels: Documentation-based analysis is not described as hands-on testing.
  • Useful limitations: Recommendations should explain trade-offs, uncertainty, and who should choose an alternative.
  • Meaningful updates: Dates change only when a page receives substantive editorial work.

Review Responsibility

AI Profit Hub does not claim a larger editorial team, professional testing laboratory, or independent benchmark programme. Review evidence and methodology are stated on the relevant page. Corrections can be submitted through the contact page.
