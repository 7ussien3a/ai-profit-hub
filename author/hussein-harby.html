?? About Me

Hello! I am Hussein Harby, the founder of AI Profit Hub. I started this blog in 2025 after realizing that the web was flooded with low-quality, AI-generated content promoting sub-par software tools just to collect affiliate commissions. I wanted to build a trusted, authority tech publication that relies entirely on first-hand experience and rigorous testing.

I spend hours every week downloading, installing, and running code on local models or calling cloud APIs to compare actual tool performance. If a tool is slow, expensive, or doesn't deliver on its promises, I will tell you directly.

?? My Editorial Principles (E-E-A-T)

Google evaluates web content using E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Here is how I apply these standards to everything I write:

  • First-Hand Experience: I never write a review based on marketing materials or summary articles. Every single tool mentioned on this blog is tested personally by me.
  • No Over-Hyped Promises: AI can speed up your work, but it's not a magic button. I showcase real limitations, bugs, and actual cost-per-task economics.
  • Continuous Updates: The AI space changes daily. I regularly update my comparison sheets, benchmarks, and guides so you always get the latest facts.

??? Verified Review Policy

AI Profit Hub maintains strict editorial independence. We do not accept paid placements to rate tools higher. Our rankings are driven purely by benchmark tests, utility assessments, and pricing transparency.
