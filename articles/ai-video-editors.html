Top 5 AI Video Editors That Will Replace Premiere Pro

📅 May 4, 2026 ✍️ Written by Hussein 📖 6 min read

Traditional video editing is a highly technical skill that takes years to master. But for content creators making YouTube videos or TikToks, you don't need Hollywood-level color grading—you need speed.

Here are the 5 best AI-powered video editors that are changing the game.

1. Descript (Text-Based Editing)

Descript transcribes your video into a text document. To edit the video, you simply delete words from the text document. Delete a sentence, and the video cuts perfectly. It’s like editing a Word document.

2. Opus Clip (The Shorts Machine)

If you record long podcasts, Opus Clip is mandatory. Paste a YouTube link, and the AI finds the most engaging 30-to-60-second clips, crops them vertically, tracks the speaker's face, and adds Alex Hormozi-style captions.

3. RunwayML (VFX in the Browser)

Runway brings Hollywood VFX to your browser. You can remove backgrounds without a green screen (rotoscoping) in one click, remove unwanted objects from a shot, or even generate video from text.

4. CapCut (The TikTok Standard)

Owned by ByteDance, CapCut is packed with AI features. Its Auto-Caption feature is industry standard, and its AI templates allow you to sync cuts to the beat of trending music automatically.

5. AutoPod (The Premiere Pro Plugin)

If you still want to use Premiere Pro, get the AutoPod plugin. It automatically edits multi-camera podcasts. It listens to who is speaking and cuts to the correct camera angle, saving hours of tedious timeline switching.
