Andrew Yang: Artificial intelligence and reducing the cost of living are the biggest opportunity for startups

✍ Hussein 📅 Last Updated: Jul 12, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read 📰 Official Source
📰 Via Official Source

Have you ever felt that your salary evaporates as soon as it reaches your bank account, and that your monthly budget has turned into a losing battle with inflation? You are not alone in this ongoing struggle, as the continuing rise in prices in all vital sectors constitutes a global challenge that worries millions of families around the world. But, what if the real solution lies not only in demanding increased income, but rather in integrating advanced technology with our basic daily needs to reduce expenses? This is exactly what former US presidential candidate and innovative entrepreneur Andrew Yang sees, as he categorically asserts that the next big opportunity for startups is not to invent new luxuries or entertainment applications, but rather to focus directly on... Artificial intelligence and reducing the cost of living.

In light of the rapid technological developments that the world is witnessing, it is clear that true innovation must redirect its compass towards the fundamental economic problems that affect the lives of ordinary individuals. In this detailed article, we will explore in depth how modern technology, especially artificial intelligence, can reshape our economies from the roots, and contribute effectively and sustainably to reducing daily expenses, and why this trend is considered the next mine of smart investments in the next decade.

How can artificial intelligence reduce the cost of living?

AI can reduce the cost of living by improving resource efficiency and reducing production costs in the housing, food, and energy sectors. By automating repetitive processes and reducing waste in supply chains, this technology allows startups to offer essential services at much lower prices, putting wasted money back into consumers' pockets and relieving daily financial burdens directly and quickly. This mechanism is not just a theory, but rather a practical application that has begun to see the light in many markets.

Andrew Yang and the new investment opportunity for startups

Andrew Yang has long been known for his bold economic ideas and his ability to see the future beyond conventional solutions. He recently pointed out a dangerous phenomenon he calls “The Great American Overpay.” The idea is that consumers today pay huge sums that are completely disproportionate to the true value of the basic services they receive, such as housing, health care, education, food, and even Internet and wireless communications services. These costs of living have alarmingly outpaced wage growth rates over the past decades.

For Yang, this apparent structural imbalance in pricing is not just a purely economic problem or a passing social crisis, but rather a historic opportunity and a veritable goldmine for technological innovation and entrepreneurship. Instead of draining minds, efforts and money focusing on developing applications for limited luxury or video games that only consume time, Yang believes that the next generation of entrepreneurs and innovators should focus all their energy on finding radical solutions aimed at what can be called “democracy of necessities.”

At its core, the idea is simple yet revolutionary: identify critical areas where people are forced to pay too much, use emerging technologies like artificial intelligence to break down these sectors, and provide the same service — or perhaps a better service — at a much lower cost. This paradigm shift is what will create the next trillion dollar companies, which will not rely on user attrition, but on saving them money.

The role of artificial intelligence and reducing the cost of living in vital sectors

Artificial intelligence is considered the primary and most important driver of this awaited economic revolution. Thanks to its superior ability to analyze huge amounts of complex data, automate tasks that previously required significant human effort, and provide accurate predictions, artificial intelligence can radically and unprecedentedly reduce operational costs across various sectors. Here's how it happens in detail in the most important sectors of life:

Cost reduction revolution in the housing and construction sector The housing crisis is one of the biggest problems facing young people and families today in big cities. But artificial intelligence intervenes to change this complex equation. Advanced machine learning algorithms can improve building designs to be more efficient, dramatically reducing waste of basic materials on construction sites, saving millions of dollars. In addition, smart energy management systems in homes help significantly reduce electricity and heating bills by continuously learning the household's daily consumption patterns and automatically adjusting temperatures and lighting. Most importantly, AI can speed up and simplify engineering permitting and blueprint review processes, reducing hidden costs and bureaucracy that are always borne by the end buyer.

Unparalleled efficiency in the food and agriculture sector The agricultural sector is currently witnessing a real revolution thanks to technology. AI-powered precision agriculture technologies are used to analyze soil quality, accurately predict weather patterns, and accurately and thoughtfully reduce the use of water, chemical fertilizers, and pesticides, significantly reducing agricultural production costs and preserving the environment. Logistics supply chain optimization systems also accurately anticipate market demand and manage inventory very efficiently from the farm to the consumer's table, which significantly reduces food waste and transportation and storage costs, which is ultimately reflected in real discounts on citizens' grocery bills.

Radical transformations in health care and preventive medicine The healthcare sector is one of the most draining sectors of personal and government budgets. Through early and accurate diagnosis of diseases using advanced artificial intelligence techniques in analyzing medical images and vital data, the need for late and expensive surgical interventions that constitute the greatest burden can be reduced. Proactive healthcare programs and personalized medicine based on DNA and medical history also reduce long-term treatment costs, saving patients thousands of dollars and saving lives at the same time.

Examples of AI startups driving change

Talking about these promising opportunities is not just futuristic theorizing or science fiction; it already exists Artificial intelligence startups It has begun to strongly embrace Andrew Yang's vision, and offers practical solutions and realistic applications that directly and significantly impact the individual and family budget:

1. Automated smart expense management platforms: They are financial applications supported by smart algorithms that analyze users’ detailed spending habits with high accuracy, to provide proactive and personalized advice aimed at reducing monthly bills. These applications are able to detect unused digital subscriptions to cancel them immediately, and even automatically negotiate via artificial intelligence intermediaries with service providers to reduce monthly fees based on competing offers in the market. 2. Modular and 3D printed building technology companies: It uses advanced artificial intelligence to design housing units that can be quickly installed or rely entirely on 3D printing technology for homes, which reduces labor costs and wasted time by up to half compared to traditional methods, and provides innovative housing solutions at affordable prices for everyone, especially for young people and new families. 3. Food waste reduction and redistribution applications: Smart platforms that connect consumers with restaurants, bakeries, and supermarkets to purchase excess food and surplus meals of high quality at very reduced prices (sometimes up to an 80% discount) at the end of the day, which contributes to directly reducing daily expenses for individuals, and reduces the harmful environmental impact resulting from food waste at the same time.

Challenges of reducing daily expenses using artificial intelligence

Despite the enormous opportunities and broad horizons that the artificial intelligence sector offers to reduce the financial burdens on individuals, the road is not paved with roses and there are a series of fundamental obstacles and challenges that must be successfully overcome to realize Andrew Yang’s promises and turn them into a tangible reality for all. The most prominent of these current challenges are:

  • **Monopoly in core sectors and institutional resistance:** Some vital sectors such as advanced healthcare, energy, and telecommunications suffer from the control of huge, monopolistic companies that may fiercely resist any emerging innovations that threaten their historical profit margins and their traditional business models that depend on keeping prices high. Overcoming this monopoly requires courageous decisions from lawmakers and support from technology companies.
  • **Initial development costs and model training:** Building effective and reliable AI models capable of delivering these cutting-edge solutions requires huge financial investments and powerful computing infrastructure, which can represent a major obstacle for independent startups in their early stages of launch and expansion.
  • **Regulatory and ethical concerns around privacy and security:** Using vast amounts of sensitive user data (such as medical or financial data) to improve services and analyze behaviors requires a high level of transparency and accountability, as well as strict and comprehensive compliance with global data protection and privacy laws to avoid complex legal consequences and loss of consumer trust.

Conclusion

Ultimately, the ambitious goal of reducing the cost of living represents the greatest challenge for the current generation and future generations, but at the same time it provides a historic investment and business opportunity that is absolutely unparalleled. By directing the wheel of innovation and creative energies towards finding smart and practical solutions to outstanding daily problems rather than focusing on the fleeting luxuries of a few, startup companies can achieve tremendous financial successes and huge profits while actually contributing in a profound positive way to improving the quality of life of millions of people around the world. The push toward a more efficient, less expensive, and more equitable economy is the logical and necessary next step for modern-day technological innovation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the biggest opportunity for startups according to Andrew Yang? Entrepreneur Andrew Yang believes that the greatest golden opportunity lies in establishing innovative startup companies that work seriously to reduce the basic costs of living for individuals, such as the costs of housing, food, health care, and education, instead of focusing only on creating luxury products that serve only a small and limited segment of society, which makes the technical benefit limited to one group without others.

How does artificial intelligence contribute to reducing the daily expenses of families in a practical way? Artificial Intelligence directly helps automate routine, repetitive tasks, improve the efficiency of use of natural resources and raw materials, and reduce waste in vital and highly influential sectors such as agriculture, energy management, and manufacturing. This comprehensive improvement of operational processes reflects positively and automatically on reducing the final cost of basic goods and services borne by the average consumer, allowing tangible savings at the end of each month.

Can artificial intelligence provide radical solutions to the ongoing global housing crisis? Although artificial intelligence does not have an integrated magic solution to eliminate the housing crisis overnight due to its complexities, its advanced and emerging technologies contribute significantly to accelerating complex construction and engineering review processes, reducing basic material and labor costs through robotics and 3D printing, and managing building energy consumption with high efficiency, which gradually and steadily helps in providing modern affordable housing options available to a much wider segment of citizens compared to the current situation.
