Andrew Yang thinks the next big startup opportunity is lowering the cost of living

✍️ Hussein 📅 June 13, 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read 📰 TechCrunch
📰 Via TechCrunch Let's be honest, the cost of living feels like it's in a perpetual arms race against your bank account. Every time you open a bill, it feels like the numbers just keep climbing, relentlessly. You’re not alone in feeling this squeeze. It's a universal experience for so many people just trying to make ends meet, or even get ahead.

The Universal Squeeze: Why Everything Feels So Expensive

It’s no secret that life is getting pricier. From the roof over your head to the food on your table and even the connection keeping you online, the costs seem to multiply. This isn't just a gut feeling; it’s a tangible reality that Andrew Yang, the entrepreneur and political figure, has been highlighting. He recently shared a really interesting perspective: Americans are consistently overpaying for the essentials. He’s talking about the big ones – housing, food, and even wireless services. Yang believes this widespread overpayment isn't just a problem; it's the next monumental opportunity for startups. Imagine a "gold rush," not for digging up precious metals, but for figuring out how to give that money back to consumers. He posits that the next wave of successful companies won't be about creating new desires, but about fulfilling fundamental needs at a fraction of the current cost. Think about it: if you can genuinely make housing 20% cheaper for millions, or drastically cut down grocery bills, you're not just building a business; you're solving a massive societal problem. It’s a compelling idea, suggesting that innovation isn't always about the flashy new gadget, but sometimes about making the mundane more affordable.

AI to the Rescue? Smart Solutions for a Leaner Life

So, if the challenge is lowering the cost of living, where does AI fit into this equation? Everywhere, actually. Artificial intelligence has the power to inject efficiency, personalization, and automation into areas that have traditionally been rife with waste, middlemen, and opaque pricing. This is exactly the kind of problem space we love to dig into at AI Profit Hub, seeing how AI tools can make a tangible difference in people's lives and create real business opportunities. Consider housing. AI can revolutionize everything from construction (think automated building techniques and predictive maintenance to reduce long-term costs) to energy efficiency (smart home systems learning your habits to slash utility bills). AI-powered platforms could help identify undervalued properties, streamline rental agreements, or even optimize shared living arrangements, making urban areas more accessible. When it comes to food, the potential is immense. AI can optimize supply chains, reducing waste from farm to fork and cutting transportation costs. Imagine AI-driven smart farms that minimize resource use, or personalized meal planning apps that not only suggest recipes but also tell you the cheapest place to buy ingredients, adapting to sales and seasonal availability. Even grocery shopping could be transformed by AI that helps you plan meals to minimize waste and maximize nutrition on a budget. And wireless or other utilities? AI already plays a role in analyzing usage patterns to recommend optimal plans, but it can go further.
