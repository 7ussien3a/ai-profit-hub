6G networks and artificial intelligence: Samsung and LG's vision

✍ Hussein 📅 Last Updated: Jul 12, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read 📰 Official Source
📰 Via Official Source

In a world where technological development is accelerating and its requirements are becoming more complex than ever before in human history, talking about fifth generation (5G) networks is no longer sufficient in any way to meet humanity's wild future ambitions. Today, in the heart of this digital revolution, global technology giants such as Samsung and LG are leading a new and fierce strategic race towards building and establishing 6G networks and artificial intelligence. This frenetic race aims to transform the concept of communications from a mere routine process of transferring data and files between connected devices to vital cognitive networks capable of self-reflection, instantaneous response, and continuous adaptation to their surrounding environment. In this comprehensive and in-depth article, we will delve into the depths of this near future to reveal what these leading companies are planning, and how these innovative and breakthrough technologies will shape the future of wireless communications for decades to come.

What is AI-Native in 6G networks?

6G networks are the eagerly awaited and anticipated sixth generation of wireless communications technologies. These networks are distinguished from all previous generations by their inherent, radical and comprehensive reliance on artificial intelligence throughout their infrastructure, a concept that is scientifically and technically called “native artificial intelligence” (AI-Native).

This means very simply that the network will not be just channels of information, but will be able to continuously learn from the historical and real-time data passing through it, adapt smoothly and intelligently to complex environmental changes, and manage itself completely independently without the need for direct human intervention. It is worth noting that this approach will allow providing ultra-high speeds and almost zero latency for unprecedented future applications such as communication via ultra-precise hologram technologies and full digital twinning of major cities and institutions.

Shifting towards native AI in 6G networks

Through previous generations of communication technologies, communication networks no longer relied solely on huge antennas and traditional towers scattered in cities, and the process of distributing spectral frequencies was no longer done manually or semi-automatically, as was the case in fourth and third generation networks. With the dawn of 6G and AI, the entire infrastructure has become an intelligent, comprehensive and interconnected entity. This innovative and revolutionary approach integrates advanced machine learning and deep learning algorithms into every layer of the underlying network, from the small peripherals and sensors carried by the user, to the massive central cloud core that processes trillions of data per second.

The primary and fundamental goal of this deep integration of artificial intelligence is not only to improve communication speed, but also to significantly reduce electrical energy consumption to enhance environmental sustainability and reduce the carbon footprint of the global communications sector. In addition, these smart networks will be able to anticipate complex technical malfunctions and fix them automatically and proactively before they occur or before the user feels any malfunction in the service. These networks also contribute to automatically redirecting available bandwidth and computing resources to meet the needs of billions of connected users and devices in real time. Through this comprehensive and intelligent approach, we are firmly and confidently paving the way for a completely new and promising phase in the development and prosperity of the future of wireless communications around the world.

Samsung's 6G vision: distributed intelligence and ubiquitous connectivity

Samsung is certainly considered one of the first leading technology companies to reveal its future plans with complete clarity and an insightful and profound vision. Samsung's vision for future 6G networks revolves around a fundamental and revolutionary idea called "Distributed Intelligence." In this innovative model, artificial intelligence is not only concentrated in huge, dispersed central servers, but rather extends its complex digital neural ramifications to cover and include every user device, every small sensor connected to the complex network, and every broadcast station, no matter how small.

* Optimizing the frequency spectrum with unprecedented efficiency: Samsung's advanced research laboratories plan to use complex artificial intelligence models to allocate spectrum frequencies and wireless resources very intelligently, significantly reducing the potential for interference and interference between different signals, and increasing the overall network capacity exponentially compared to what we see today. * Immersive experiences that go beyond the limits of imagined reality: The company confidently and proudly expects that the speed of the sixth generation will provide complete sensory and interactive experiences such as ultra-high-definition virtual reality (VR) and comprehensive augmented reality (AR), in addition to holograms of people in real time and with amazing quality that conveys the feeling and minute details as if people are sitting with you in the exact same room. * Micro advanced digital twin: The company aims to build highly accurate digital copies and twins of the real world (Digital Twins) to monitor and improve operational performance in completely safe virtual environments governed by artificial intelligence before implementing it on the ground. This amazing development serves huge and vital sectors such as heavy industry, smart city management, and future infrastructure planning.

LG Efforts: Intelligent Cloud-Based Communications

On the other side of the global technology landscape, the Korean company LG has not been sitting idly by in this strategic technology arms race. On the contrary, in its ongoing development of 6G technologies and networks, LG is focusing intensely and significantly on deep and close cooperation with the most prestigious academic institutions, universities and research around the world. Through this cooperation, the company aims to build flexible and scalable communication platforms that rely entirely on open cloud architecture, supported and operated by the latest artificial intelligence algorithms.

LG is constantly seeking to integrate and harmonize the unlimited capabilities of cloud computing with what is known as very precise edge computing located on the edges of the network (Edge Computing). The goal of this integration is to reduce response time and transfer data to infinitesimal fractions of a second, almost imperceptible to humans. This advanced strategic direction will make it possible, practically and reliably, to control precise medical robots that perform surgeries, fully autonomous vehicles that roam crowded city streets, and drones in a much safer, more stable and reliable way than is possible with current communications technology.

Sixth generation speed: Do we really need this imaginary amount of speed?

When talking and delving into the details and numbers of the fantastic speed of the sixth generation, we are talking about terrifying and unprecedented data transfer speeds that may reach 1 terabyte per second (1 Tbps). This is theoretically and practically at least 50 times faster than the peak 5G connection speeds available in the best and ideal conditions today. But the logical and legitimate question asked by many in technical circles and among the general public: Do we, as humans and ordinary users, really need this very tremendous speed that exceeds our current imaginations?

The short, direct and emphatic answer is absolutely yes. However, it is not necessarily for browsing traditional websites, browsing social media platforms, or even watching high-quality videos (4K or 8K). These usual daily needs are covered by the fifth generation and below with complete efficiency. Extreme speed combined with near-zero response time (Microsecond Latency) is a critical infrastructure requirement and essential for heavy industrial, sensitive medical, and complex future scientific applications.

Imagine, for example, a skilled surgeon and specialist located on a different continent from his patient, performing a complex and very critical open-heart surgery using a surgical robot connected to the Internet. Any delay in response or signal transmission, even for one millisecond, could be catastrophic and directly threaten the patient's life. Specifically here, the urgent and urgent need for stable 6G networks fully supported by artificial intelligence emerges to ensure that communication remains perfect and free of any delay, whatever the circumstances.

The impact of 6G networks on the healthcare and telemedicine sector

One of the most vital sectors that will benefit incredibly and exceptionally from the leap of 6G networks and artificial intelligence is the advanced healthcare sector and the field of telemedicine. Thanks to the extremely high speed and response time that does not exceed milliseconds, doctors and surgeons will be able to interact fully, directly and realistically with 3D images (Holograms) of patients and their vital organs in real time and with extreme accuracy.

Moreover, the anticipated sixth generation will facilitate the complete and safe adoption of highly accurate bio-sensors that can be implanted inside the human body or worn as daily attachments to monitor the patient’s comprehensive health status instantly and permanently. These sensors will collect and analyze vital signs using artificial intelligence capabilities without any significant time delay, which means the possibility of proactive medical intervention before sudden health crises occur. This qualitative and radical shift will make superior medical care possible and accessible even in rural and remote areas, provided that the necessary and basic coverage of these strong future networks is available.

The impact of 6G networks on smart industries and the Internet of Things

As the Internet of Things (IoT) develops into a broader and more comprehensive concept that may be called the “Internet of Senses” in the 6G era, various devices will be able not only to communicate and exchange data, but also to simulate the senses of smell, touch, and taste digitally in an amazing way. The heavy industrial sector will witness a major and radical transformation through the construction of smart factories that rely completely and comprehensively on autonomous robots and artificial intelligence to coordinate complex production lines and logistics with unprecedented flexibility and very high efficiency.

Native AI networks embedded in every inch of these factories will make it possible to track millions of parts, tools and devices with extreme accuracy and in real time without any interruption. This will naturally improve the management of complex global supply chains, and significantly reduce material and time waste, raising the efficiency of industrial processes to record levels that humanity has never witnessed before.

Information security challenges in the sixth generation

With all these expected and amazing positives, the biggest and most dangerous challenge facing the future of wireless communications and its rapid development remains ensuring absolute information security and protecting the digital privacy of users. With high speeds, total and fundamental dependence on artificial intelligence, and the constant and intense interconnectedness of everything around us, networks and devices connected to them become more vulnerable to complex and sophisticated cyber attacks.

Therefore, 6G networks and artificial intelligence will inevitably require the development of highly advanced security encryption systems that operate independently, and it may require the integration of complex quantum cryptography techniques to ensure the protection of sensitive user data, and to strongly and effectively confront organized hacking attempts in which hackers and cybercriminals may use quantum computers and counter-artificial intelligence to break traditional security barriers.

Contrary to what is heavily marketed and promoted in glamorous technical conferences and commercial advertisements, I personally see, clearly and frankly, that the current 6G network race is merely a display of competitive technological strength between major companies rather than a real and radical solution to the fundamental problems we suffer from in the communications sector today. The continued excessive focus on achieving astronomical numbers such as 1 terabyte per second speed unfortunately ignores the alarmingly widening global digital divide, as current 5G networks still suffer from extremely weak coverage and lack of reliability even in many cities in developed countries. The real and tangible indicator of the maturity and success of this expected technology will never be in the abstract theoretical speed numbers that we read in the news, but rather will lie in the ability of these technical companies to build artificial intelligence models that are actually and practically capable of managing networks themselves to reduce the physical operating cost to the end user, and make high-quality communications available, stable and reliable for everyone, and not just limited to complex industrial environments and elite smart cities that are geographically and financially limited.

Conclusion: The future awaits us and is open to possibilities

In conclusion, the robust research reports and initiatives launched by Samsung and LG are courageous and bold steps that clearly define and define the future of wireless communications for the coming decades. It is expected and strongly agreed upon by most experts in this field that the actual commercial launch of 6G technologies will begin in the early 2030s (around 2030). By that expected time, our usual smartphones will no longer be the main center of communication and communication as we know them today. Rather, the baton will pass to smart wearable devices and the smart environments surrounding us that will interact with us smoothly and with a deep understanding of our needs. Frequently asked questions about 6G networks

When will 6G networks be officially launched commercially around the world? Expectations and studies issued by research initiatives from major international companies such as Samsung and LG indicate that the initial and actual commercial launch of 6G networks will be expected in the period between 2028 and 2030, with wider global spread a few years later.

What is the fundamental role of AI in the 6G architecture? Unlike previous generations where AI was just an external add-on tool for improvement and efficiency, AI in 6G is not an add-on feature that can be abandoned, but rather an essential, inherent and integral part of the infrastructure (AI-Native). It performs a major task in managing the entire network, allocating frequencies with complete flexibility, and predicting potential faults and automatically fixing them before they cause any interruption to users.

Will current phones and smart devices support connection to 6G networks? No, due to the radical and significant difference in the spectral frequencies used and the complex data processing techniques that are highly required in this new generation, effective connection to 6G networks will require the purchase of completely new smart devices and processors designed and manufactured specifically to deal with this advanced and amazing generation of networks and very high frequencies.
