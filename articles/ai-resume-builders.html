The Best AI Resume Builders to Land Your Dream Job

📅 May 4, 2026 ✍️ Written by Hussein 📖 6 min read

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) before a human HR manager ever sees them. To beat the bots, you need to use a bot.

1. Rezi.ai (The ATS Beater)

Rezi is specifically designed to get past ATS software. You paste the job description you are applying for, and Rezi highlights the exact keywords missing from your resume and uses AI to help you write bullet points that include those words naturally.

2. Kickresume

If you have a blank page and don't know where to start, Kickresume is perfect. It uses OpenAI’s models to generate the first draft of your resume based on your job title alone, which you can then edit and refine.

3. The DIY ChatGPT Method

You don't necessarily need a paid tool. You can use this prompt in ChatGPT: "Here is my current resume: [Paste]. Here is the job description I want to apply for: [Paste]. Rewrite my resume bullet points to highlight my experience as it relates directly to the requirements in the job description. Use strong action verbs and include metrics where possible."
