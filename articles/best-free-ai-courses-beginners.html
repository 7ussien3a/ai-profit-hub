The 7 best free artificial intelligence courses for beginners 2026

✍ Hussein 📅 Last Updated: Jul 12, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read 📰 Official Source
📰 Via Official Source

Written by: Hussein Harby

Are you looking for the ideal starting point to enter the world of artificial intelligence full of opportunities without incurring high costs? With the rapid and amazing development of technology and the widespread proliferation of large language models such as ChatGPT and Gemini, it has become Learn artificial intelligence from scratch An indispensable necessity for every programmer, entrepreneur, and person interested in innovation.

Companies today in all sectors are eagerly looking for talent capable of understanding and employing artificial intelligence tools in different work environments to increase productivity and reduce costs. Whether you're looking to change your career path or enhance your existing skills, getting the right start is key. In this comprehensive guide, we will review the best free artificial intelligence courses for beginners that will put you on the right path to building a bright career that keeps pace with the rapid changes in the technical labor market.

What are the best free AI courses for beginners? Courses like Andrew Ng's AI for Everyone on Coursera, Google Cloud's Generative AI Introduction track, and Harvard's popular CS50's Introduction to AI with Python course are some of the best free AI courses for beginners available today. These courses provide a basic and comprehensive understanding of core concepts without the need for previous programming experience and lay the first building block for professionalism.

1. AI for Everyone course (provided by DeepLearning.AI)

The “AI for Everyone” course, presented by the leading global expert Andrew Ng on the Coursera platform, is one of the most important introductions to understanding artificial intelligence worldwide. This course is not intended for engineers and programmers only, but rather targets business leaders, product managers, marketers, and anyone who wants to understand how artificial intelligence works and its profound impact on various industries in the near future.

In this course, you will learn basic terms in a very simplified way, such as Machine Learning and Deep Learning, and how to build and manage successful artificial intelligence projects within organizations. Most importantly, you'll learn about the limits of AI and what it can't do, helping you make realistic expectations and avoid misinformation. If you are looking for a non-technical starting point that provides you with a comprehensive strategic vision, this course is your undisputed first choice. You can access it via the Coursera website and start learning completely free.

2. Google Generative AI Learning Path courses

In light of the great revolution brought about by advanced artificial intelligence models, Google launched a comprehensive and free educational path through the Google Cloud Skills Boost platform. Google artificial intelligence courses Today, it is considered one of the most powerful free sources for understanding generative artificial intelligence technology and its various practical applications in daily life and work fields. The official source of Google It provides you with the latest updates in this field.

The course begins with a simple, easy-to-understand introduction to generative AI, then goes on to explain how large language models (LLMs) work, and how to write Prompt Engineering to get the best results. The nice thing about this path is that it offers you small certificates of completion or digital badges that you can add to your LinkedIn profile to support your career path. The courses are carefully designed to be quick to digest, and use practical examples from multiple Google products, making them ideal for those who want to apply these concepts in building modern, smart, cloud-based applications.

3. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python (CS50's Intro to AI - Harvard)

If you have a simple background in programming and aspire to dive deeper into the technical and programming side, Harvard University's famous CS50 course dedicated to artificial intelligence with Python is a real treasure that you should not miss. This course skillfully moves you from a mere user of ready-made tools to an engineer capable of understanding artificial intelligence algorithms from the inside and building models from scratch.

The course covers advanced topics in a simplified manner, such as search algorithms, graphs, machine learning, natural language processing, and artificial neural networks. Although it is more complex and requires more effort than previous courses, it offers incomparable academic and practical value and represents a solid bridge towards obtaining... Accredited artificial intelligence certificates for advanced level later. Through extensive hands-on projects, you will be able to program artificial intelligence systems capable of making autonomous decisions and solving complex problems. The course is available for free to everyone via the edX platform.

4. Microsoft AI Fundamentals

Through its famous educational platform Microsoft Learn, Microsoft offers an excellent path for beginners that focuses on the basic concepts of artificial intelligence and how to apply them practically using Azure cloud services. This course is absolutely ideal for those who plan to work in corporate environments that rely on Microsoft solutions and want to understand applications such as Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Conversational AI.

What really sets this course apart is the focus on the applied, business, and ethical side of AI in a real business environment. It also directly prepares you to pass the AI-900 certification exam, one of the most recognized and highly sought-after foundational certifications in the technical job market. The course provides simple reading texts, interactive videos, and practical cloud laboratories that allow you to experience artificial intelligence techniques and train models directly without the complexities of setting up a development environment on your personal computer.

5. Kaggle Intro to Machine Learning course for beginners

Kaggle, currently a subsidiary of Google, is the world's largest community of data scientists and AI learners. Through their Kaggle Courses section, they offer a free, quick and very practical course called “Introduction to Machine Learning.” This course is specially designed for those who prefer to learn through direct practical application and writing code from day one without prolonging the theoretical aspect.

The course takes you on a short but very intense journey to learn how to explore and analyze data, build the first predictive models using popular Python libraries such as Scikit-Learn, and periodically evaluate and improve the performance of these models. The course is completely based on the in-browser working environment (Kaggle Notebooks), which means that you will not need to install any complex programs or libraries on your computer, and you can execute the codes and see the interactive results immediately. It is the ideal and fastest choice for anyone who wants to enter the field of data science and machine learning with confidence.

6. IBM Generative AI Basics

IBM, a long-established and veteran company in the world of technology, offers a valuable set of free courses that focus specifically on generative artificial intelligence and its direct impact on the business sector and major companies. The courses available through global educational platforms such as Coursera and edX are characterized by academic and professional depth and a focus on institutional applications of artificial intelligence to solve complex problems, which is what major companies are currently urgently looking for.

The various educational materials include comprehensive explanations on how to use artificial intelligence models to improve employees’ daily productivity, automate routine and repetitive tasks, and build smart and effective chatbots for customer service. IBM also strongly highlights the importance of governance and ethical controls in AI to ensure that these new technologies are used responsibly and safely. Gaining knowledge and guidance from a leading and trusted organization like IBM adds significant weight to your professional profile and enhances your comprehensive understanding of industry challenges.

7. Artificial Intelligence for Business from the University of Pennsylvania (AI for Business)

If your main goal is... Learn artificial intelligence from scratch Whether it is used to increase your company's profits, improve operational processes, or develop modern management, the "AI for Business" path offered by the prestigious Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania is the most appropriate and beneficial path for you. This specialized track focuses exclusively on the management, financial, and strategic aspects, and explains with real examples how artificial intelligence can change the rules of the competitive game in vital areas such as digital marketing, finance, and human resources.

Through this comprehensive course, large parts of which are freely available to the public, you will clearly learn how to build an integrated and sustainable AI strategy for your company, and how to manage technical teams and data scientists effectively and efficiently even if you do not have a prior programming background. This course is an indispensable guide for senior executives, investors, and aspiring entrepreneurs seeking to achieve a real and sustainable competitive advantage in the era of the new digital economy powered entirely by artificial intelligence.

Conclusion

Starting your educational journey with the best free AI courses for beginners is undoubtedly the smartest strategic investment you can make of your time and effort in 2026 and beyond. Whether you choose to explore the business and management path with Andrew Ng's course, delve into the technical and programming side with the prestigious Harvard University, or explore the latest findings in Google's artificial intelligence courses in the field of generative models, the most important step is to start today and persevere in continuous learning.

Advanced knowledge has become available to everyone without exception, and the real competitive advantage will go exclusively to those who apply this knowledge intelligently and quickly. Don't let this historic opportunity pass you by and take advantage of these golden educational resources to build an impressive and sustainable career.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn AI without prior programming background? Yes, absolutely. Many foundational courses for beginners, such as the “AI for Everyone” course, are specifically designed to explain complex concepts and basic principles without the need to write a single line of code. Later, you can learn languages ​​like Python if you want to go deeper into building models.

What are the best accredited artificial intelligence certificates for beginners in the job market? The Microsoft AI-900 (Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals) certification, Google Cloud specialized certifications, and Coursera course completion certificates from DeepLearning.AI are among the best foundational certifications that strongly support your CV and prove your seriousness to employers.

How long does it take to learn the basics of artificial intelligence and start applying it? The length of time varies greatly depending on your personal commitment and the level of the course you choose. Very simple foundation courses may take only one week to one month (an average of a few hours of study per week), while integrated academic tracks such as the Harvard CS50 course may take from 10 to 12 weeks to understand the technical aspects well and build strong projects.
