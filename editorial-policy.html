Editorial Policy

Our commitment to honesty, accuracy, and editorial integrity.

Editorial purpose

AI Profit Hub publishes practical English-language guides, comparisons, reviews, and news analysis about artificial intelligence products. Our goal is to help readers understand documented product differences and make better decisions without presenting marketing claims as independent evidence.

Accuracy and source standards

  • Primary sources first: Product specifications, release details, access, and pricing should link to official documentation, release notes, research papers, or company announcements whenever available.
  • Independent reporting: Reputable secondary reporting may be used for transactions, policy discussions, or disputed events that have no complete primary record.
  • Attribution: Provider benchmarks and company claims are identified as publisher claims. They are not described as AI Profit Hub test results.
  • Uncertainty: Rumours, forecasts, and incomplete reports must be clearly labelled or excluded. Unsupported claims are removed from indexable content.

Review and comparison labels

Most current reviews are documentation-based reviews. They evaluate official product information, pricing, privacy documentation, access methods, and practical use cases. A page may claim hands-on testing only when it states the test date, plan or version, method, and evaluation criteria. See How We Review AI Tools for the full methodology.

Editorial independence and monetisation

Advertisers do not choose topics or approve conclusions. AI Profit Hub may display automated advertising. A commercial relationship, affiliate link, or sponsored placement must be disclosed on the relevant page. The presence of an advertisement is not an endorsement of the advertiser.

Corrections and meaningful updates

Readers can report an error through the contact page. Material factual corrections should include a dated correction note when they change the meaning or recommendation. Publication and update dates are changed only when substantive editorial work has occurred.

Use of AI in publishing

AI tools may assist with outlining, language editing, structured extraction, code checks, or research organisation. AI output is not accepted as a source. Indexable pages require editorial review, source checks, and a final human decision. Pages that have not completed that process are removed from search and sitemap discovery until they are ready.

Review responsibility

Hussein Harby is the named editor responsible for the current publication. AI Profit Hub does not claim a larger testing laboratory, specialist staff, or independent benchmark programme where those resources do not exist.
