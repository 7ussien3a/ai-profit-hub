About OpenAI

Founded in 2015 as a non-profit artificial intelligence research laboratory, OpenAI has grown to become the most influential force in the modern AI landscape. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, OpenAI's stated mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity.

The organization transitioned to a "capped-profit" model in 2019 to secure the massive computational resources and funding required to train state-of-the-art models, leading to a major multi-billion dollar partnership with Microsoft. With the release of ChatGPT in late 2022, OpenAI sparked the global generative AI revolution, establishing itself as the gold standard for conversational AI and large language models.

??? Flagship Products & Models

ChatGPT AI Assistant

The world's leading generative AI platform. ChatGPT allows users to write, brainstorm, code, analyze data, and learn through a natural chat interface. Supports custom GPTs and voice interactions.

GPT-4o & GPT-4 LLM

OpenAI's state-of-the-art multimodal reasoning models. Capable of understanding and responding to text, audio, and visual inputs in real-time with human-like responsiveness.

DALL-E 3 Image AI

A highly advanced text-to-image generator that translates complex descriptions into detailed, high-resolution visual art, integrated natively into ChatGPT Plus.

Sora Video AI

OpenAI's revolutionary text-to-video model that creates realistic and imaginative video scenes up to 60 seconds long from natural language prompts.

? Frequently Asked Questions

What is OpenAI's most popular product? ?
OpenAI's most popular product is ChatGPT, an AI-powered conversational assistant launched in November 2022. It is built on top of OpenAI's GPT-4o, GPT-4, and GPT-3.5 large language models.
Is ChatGPT free to use? ?
Yes, OpenAI offers a free version of ChatGPT utilizing the GPT-4o mini model. They also offer a paid subscription plan called ChatGPT Plus for $20/month, which provides access to advanced features, higher usage limits for GPT-4o, custom GPTs, and advanced data analysis.
What are OpenAI's latest models? ?
OpenAI's latest flagship models include GPT-4o (a native multimodal model capable of real-time audio, vision, and text processing), GPT-4o mini (a cost-efficient small model), and GPT-4. They also have specialized models like DALL-E 3 for image generation and Sora for text-to-video generation.
