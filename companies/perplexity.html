🟢 Perplexity

Redefining web search with real-time citations, Pro Search reasoning, and interactive AI research assistants.

About Perplexity AI

Founded in 2022 by former engineers from OpenAI, Meta, and Quora (including CEO Aravind Srinivas), Perplexity AI was created to solve a key limitation of traditional search engines: sorting through pages of blue links. Perplexity acts as an answer engine that reads the web for you, writes a synthesized summary, and points directly to the sources via inline numbers.

Perplexity has seen exponential growth by focusing entirely on the "search" paradigm rather than general conversational chat. Backed by prominent tech figures like Jeff Bezos and NVIDIA, Perplexity has established itself as the leading AI-first competitor to Google Search, offering users a cleaner, ad-free research environment.

🛠️ Flagship Products & Models

Pro Search Deep Research

An advanced search mode that performs multi-step web queries, asks clarifying questions to narrow down intent, and aggregates research from dozens of websites.

Source Citations Transparency

Direct inline numbers linking to the exact web pages used to synthesize the answer. Allows users to verify facts, cross-reference data, and avoid AI hallucinations.

Perplexity Pages Authoring

A feature allowing users to turn any web research topic into a beautifully formatted, structured, and publishable article or guide with a single click.

File & Code Analysis AI Workspace

Allows users to upload PDFs, CSVs, or text files directly. The AI can parse code, write mathematical summaries, and extract insights using real-time search context.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does Perplexity AI work? ▼
Perplexity AI is a conversational search engine that answers queries by scanning the web in real-time, summarizing findings, and providing direct inline citations. It utilizes advanced large language models like GPT-4, Claude 3.5, and its own fine-tuned models to summarize information.
Is Perplexity free to use? ▼
Yes, Perplexity offers a free tier that gives users access to basic conversational search. They also offer 'Perplexity Pro' for $20/month, which unlocks 'Pro Search' (deep multi-step research), unlimited file uploads, and the ability to choose your preferred AI model (e.g., Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o).
What is Perplexity Pages? ▼
Perplexity Pages is a content generation feature that lets users convert search results into beautifully formatted, publishable articles, reports, or guides. It designs layouts and gathers images automatically based on user prompts.
