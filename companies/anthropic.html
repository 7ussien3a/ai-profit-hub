🟤 Anthropic

Building helpful, honest, and harmless AI systems through Constitutional safety and pioneering Claude models.

About Anthropic

Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers (including siblings Daniela and Dario Amodei), Anthropic was established as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) with a singular focus on building safe, steerable, and reliable AI systems. Anthropic pioneered the concept of Constitutional AI—a training method that aligns AI behavior with structured safety principles rather than subjective human feedback.

Backed by major investments from tech giants Amazon (providing AWS compute) and Google, Anthropic has emerged as OpenAI's closest technological rival. Its Claude model family is widely praised by developers for its coding capabilities, nuanced writing style, and large context windows, making it a favorite for enterprise applications.

🛠️ Flagship Products & Models

Claude AI AI Assistant

Anthropic's consumer web assistant. Highly regarded for its natural writing tone, analytical reasoning, and safety. Includes Claude Artifacts for live code rendering.

Claude 3.5 & 4 LLM Family

State-of-the-art models including Claude 3.5 Sonnet (the developer gold standard for coding and logical reasoning) and Claude 3.5 Haiku (an ultra-fast, cost-efficient model).

Claude Artifacts Workspace

A dedicated interactive panel that runs alongside the chat, allowing users to view, edit, and interact with generated code, websites, SVGs, and markdown in real-time.

Constitutional safety Research

Innovative research frameworks ensuring AI systems remain aligned, harmless, and transparent, setting safety benchmarks for the entire AI industry.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Claude AI? ▼
Claude AI is Anthropic's flagship family of large language models. Claude is known for high-quality writing, advanced coding, logical reasoning, and possessing a large context window. It also features a unique 'Artifacts' interactive workspace.
Is Claude free to use? ▼
Yes, Anthropic offers a free tier of Claude on their web platform. For power users, they offer 'Claude Pro' for $20/month, providing 5x more usage limits, priority access during peak hours, and early access to new features and models.
What makes Anthropic different from OpenAI? ▼
Anthropic was founded with a Public Benefit Charter specifically focusing on safety and transparency. They developed Constitutional AI, a method that uses rules (a constitution) to guide model alignment, rather than relying solely on human reinforcement, making Claude models highly robust and reliable.
