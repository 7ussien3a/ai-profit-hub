🔵 Google AI

Pioneering AI research with Gemini multimodal models, Gemma open-source models, and Google DeepMind innovations.

About Google AI & DeepMind

Google has been an "AI-first" company since 2016, leading fundamental research that underpins the entire modern generative AI wave—including the invention of the Transformer architecture in 2017. In 2023, Google consolidated its two primary AI research divisions, Google Brain and DeepMind, into a single unit called Google DeepMind, headed by Demis Hassabis.

Google's current AI strategy revolves around its native multimodal model family, Gemini, which is deeply integrated across its billions-of-users consumer ecosystems like Android, Google Search (SGE/AI Overviews), Chrome, and Google Workspace. Google is also a pioneer in open-source AI with the Gemma model family, and AI-native hardware with its custom Tensor Processing Units (TPUs).

🛠️ Flagship Products & Models

Gemini AI Assistant

Google's central conversational agent. Available on web, iOS, and natively integrated on Android as the system assistant. Capable of analyzing complex documents, code, and images.

Gemini 1.5 & 2.0 Models

Flagship multimodal reasoning engines featuring an industry-leading 1 million to 2 million token context window, allowing users to process hours of video or entire codebases in a single prompt.

Gemma Open Source

A family of lightweight open-weights models built for developers and researchers, capable of running efficiently on local consumer hardware (laptops, edge devices).

Veo & Imagen Creative AI

Google's highly advanced generative media models. Veo generates high-quality 1080p video clips, while Imagen 3 produces photo-realistic, text-accurate images.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Google Gemini? ▼
Google Gemini is Google's flagship family of highly capable multimodal AI models, replacing the previous Bard and Duet AI brands. Gemini is integrated into Google Search, Android, Google Workspace, and is available as a dedicated web assistant and mobile application.
Is Google Gemini free to use? ▼
Yes, Google offers a free version of Gemini utilizing the Gemini Flash model. They also offer a premium subscription plan called Gemini Advanced (part of the Google One AI Premium Plan) for $19.99/month, which provides access to Gemini Ultra/Pro models, larger context windows, and integrations with Docs, Gmail, and Drive.
What is Google Gemma? ▼
Gemma is a family of lightweight, state-of-the-art open-source models built by Google from the same research and technology used to create the Gemini models. Gemma models are designed for developers and researchers to run locally on laptops or desktops.
